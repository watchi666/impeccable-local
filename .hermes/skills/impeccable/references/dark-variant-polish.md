# Dark-variant polish

Use this when polishing dark or cinematic preview lanes.

## Remove dead voids
- Large empty dark areas next to important copy are usually not mood, they are missing composition.
- Fill them with a context-true supporting figure, image, or authored material move.

## Avoid pasted-in white CTA slabs
- Do not end a dark page with a generic white contact block.
- Keep the contact surface inside the page's dark material system and preserve emphasis with accent color, scale, and contrast.

## Reduce permanently visible sticky headers
- If the header sits over the hero all the time, reduce or hide it away from the top.
- Let it return when the pointer or scroll behavior justifies it.

## Recover image vitality
- Check for luminosity blending, grayscale washes, low opacity, heavy brightness reduction, and overlay layers.
- Remove damping before adding styling.
- Then restore local copy readability with targeted text shadow or a compact local backdrop.
