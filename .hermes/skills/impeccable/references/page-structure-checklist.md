# Page structure checklist

Use this before claiming craft is complete.

- [ ] Header with navigation and visible contact
- [ ] Hero with dominant real image, strong statement, CTA
- [ ] Positioning section with real flowing text
- [ ] Services section with at least three concrete offer points
- [ ] Proof section with three to four real images and non-grid rhythm
- [ ] Trust/locality section with address and local credibility
- [ ] Contact section with phone, email, address, and low-friction CTA
- [ ] At least six to eight real photos across the page
- [ ] Visual direction stays consistent from hero to footer
