# Scroll dramaturgy vs anti-grid

## Good scroll dramaturgy
- Each act answers the next customer question.
- Density and scale change deliberately.
- Proof arrives early enough to build trust.
- Contact feels earned and inevitable.

## False anti-grid
- Sections are visually smeared together but still mentally empty.
- Random overlaps replace hierarchy.
- Everything feels equally important.
- Contact lands as leftover admin.

## Rule
Anti-grid means softer seams and less visible modularity. It does not mean no structure, no acts, or no trust-building sequence.
